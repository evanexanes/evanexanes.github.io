03 — How I work

Built to be handed over

A site I finish is a site someone else can run. That means named custom fields instead of hard-coded text, a template for anything that repeats, and a short written handover so the next person is not reverse-engineering my work.

It also means the site is legible to machines. Structured data describes what a page is rather than leaving a crawler to guess from the layout, slugs stay stable so links do not rot, and redirects resolve in one hop instead of a chain. That work is invisible on the page and decides whether the page is quoted at all — which is what AEO and GEO actually mean: being readable to the assistant answering the question, not only to the search index.
